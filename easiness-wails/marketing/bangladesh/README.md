# Easiness — Bangladesh Marketing Strategy

This folder contains everything you need to market and sell Easiness in Bangladesh.

It is designed for someone with no prior marketing experience.
Read this file first, then follow the steps below.

---

## Why Bangladesh is a strong market for Easiness

| Factor | Why it helps Easiness |
|--------|----------------------|
| Most small businesses use paper or basic Excel | No strong competitor already in place |
| Unreliable internet and frequent power cuts | Offline-first is a genuine selling point — not just a feature |
| bKash and Nagad are everywhere | Low-friction digital payments with no credit card required |
| Facebook penetration is extremely high | Cheap, targeted advertising to business owners |
| WhatsApp and Messenger are widely used | Direct outreach works well |
| Low software spending culture | Very low price point removes the main objection |
| Strong trading, wholesale, garments, and retail sectors | All three target segments exist in large numbers |

---

## Pricing model for Bangladesh

Bangladesh uses a different pricing structure from the global pricing.

The base price is very low — designed to make the "no thanks" answer difficult.
The sync add-on is where the price increases, because it adds real server costs and value.

See `context/pricing-bangladesh.md` for full details.

**Quick summary:**

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | What's different |
|------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| Offline (1 device) | ৳60/month | ৳600/year | Core features, no internet needed |
| Sync (multi-device) | ৳180/month | ৳1,800/year | Cloud sync, browser access |
| Team (multi-user) | ৳360/month | ৳3,600/year | Team access, priority support |

## Language

- For Segment 1 (retail shops): **write in Bengali (Bangla)**. Shop owners respond better to their native language.
- For Segment 2 and 3 (wholesale, trading): **English is acceptable** but Bengali increases trust.
- The prompt files include instructions for both languages.

---

## Where to focus first

1. Start in **Dhaka** — highest concentration of small businesses, most connected
2. Then **Chittagong** — major trading and import/export hub
3. Then **Sylhet** — strong retail and trading community with diaspora connections

## Step-by-step launch plan for Bangladesh

### Week 1–2: Prepare
- [ ] Read all files in `context/`
- [ ] Read the customer segment profiles in `customers/`
- [ ] Set up a Facebook Business page for Easiness
- [ ] Set up a WhatsApp Business account with your business profile
- [ ] Create a bKash and Nagad merchant account for payments
- [ ] Get 5–10 screenshots of the product (stock screen, invoice screen, dashboard)

### Week 3–4: First content
- [ ] Use prompts in `prompts/segment-1-retail/` to generate Facebook posts
- [ ] Post 3 times per week on Facebook
- [ ] Join 5–10 Facebook groups for Bangladeshi small business owners (see `context/market-overview.md`)
- [ ] Share posts manually in those groups (with permission)

### Week 5–6: First outreach
- [ ] Use `prompts/segment-1-retail/whatsapp-broadcast.md` to write WhatsApp messages
- [ ] Reach out to 20–30 shop owners in your local area or network
- [ ] Offer a free 14-day trial to the first 10 responders
- [ ] Ask for feedback and a testimonial in exchange

### Week 7+: Scale and Segment 2
- [ ] Use testimonials from Segment 1 as proof in Segment 2 outreach
- [ ] Begin LinkedIn and email outreach to wholesale and trading companies
- [ ] List Easiness on local software directories (see `context/market-overview.md`)
