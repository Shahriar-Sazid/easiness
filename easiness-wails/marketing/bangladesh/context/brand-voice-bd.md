# Brand Voice — Bangladesh Adaptation

This file adapts the global brand voice (`marketing/context/brand-voice.md`) for the Bangladesh market.
Read the global brand voice first, then use this file for Bangladesh-specific adjustments.

---

## Core brand personality (unchanged)

Easiness is like a **trusted, smart friend who understands your business**.
This holds in Bangladesh. The trusted-friend tone translates directly to Bangladeshi culture,
where personal relationships and trust matter more than corporate polish.

---

## Bangladesh-specific tone adjustments

### For Segment 1 (Retail shops) — Bengali content

| Global guideline | Bangladesh adjustment |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Clear, simple language | Use everyday Bangla — not formal/literary Bangla. Write how people talk in a shop. |
| Confident | Do not be boastful. Bangladeshi buyers distrust overselling. Let the feature speak. |
| Empathetic | Name the exact pain. "খাতায় হিসাব ভুল হয়" (ledger calculations go wrong) is better than a generic statement. |

**Bengali register to use:**
- Use চলিত ভাষা (everyday spoken Bengali) not সাধু ভাষা (formal written Bengali)
- Address the reader as "আপনি" (respectful you) — not "তুমি"
- Use short sentences. One idea per sentence.

**Example of the difference:**

Wrong (too formal):
> "Easiness সফটওয়্যারটি আপনার ব্যবসায়িক কার্যক্রম সুচারুভাবে পরিচালনা করতে সহায়তা করিবে।"

Right (natural, conversational):
> "দোকানের স্টক, কেনাকাটা, বিক্রয় — সব এক জায়গায় রাখুন। হিসাব ভুল হবে না।"

### For Segment 2 and 3 (Wholesale, Trading) — English or mixed

- English is acceptable and often expected for professional B2B communication
- For email and LinkedIn: write in English
- For Facebook posts targeting these segments: Bengali or a natural Bengali-English mix works well

**Bangladesh-specific English tone:**
- Direct and respectful
- Mention local context where possible: "Managing supplier accounts across Chittagong port" resonates more than a generic phrase
- Avoid overly Western idioms that may feel unfamiliar

## Customer language — Bangladesh version

These are real phrases Bangladeshi small business owners use.
Use these exact words in your copy.

**In Bengali:**
- "খাতায় হিসাব মেলে না" (the ledger doesn't balance)
- "মাল আছে কি না জানি না" (I don't know if the goods are in stock)
- "কোন সাপ্লায়ারকে কত দেওয়া বাকি মনে নেই" (I can't remember how much I owe each supplier)
- "লাভ হচ্ছে কি না বুঝতে পারছি না" (I can't tell if I'm making profit)
- "Excel-এ সব গেড়ে যায়" (everything gets messed up in Excel)
- "মাস শেষে হিসাব মেলাতে ঘণ্টার পর ঘণ্টা লাগে" (takes hours to reconcile accounts at month end)
- "কাস্টমার বলে দিয়েছি, কিন্তু রেকর্ড নেই" (customer says they paid, but I have no record)

**In English (for B2B segments):**
- "I have no idea which products are actually making me money"
- "Reconciling supplier invoices at month end takes a whole day"
- "My staff can't see the same data I see from the office"
- "One disputed invoice from an overseas supplier tied up our cash for months"

## Words and phrases TO USE in Bangladesh

- "এক জায়গায়" (one place) — simplicity
- "নিজে থেকে আপডেট হয়" (updates automatically) — no manual work
- "ইন্টারনেট ছাড়াও চলে" (works without internet) — offline advantage
- "আপনার কম্পিউটারে" (on your computer) — data privacy
- "মাসিক মাত্র ৳60" (only ৳60 per month) — price anchor
- "bKash-এ পেমেন্ট করুন" (pay via bKash) — familiar payment
- "লাভ দেখাবে তাৎক্ষণিকভাবে" (shows profit instantly)
- "কোনো হিসাবের ভুল নেই" (no calculation errors)

## Words to AVOID in Bangladesh

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| "ক্লাউড-ভিত্তিক সমাধান" (cloud-based solution) | Jargon; sounds like it requires constant internet | "যেকোনো ডিভাইস থেকে দেখা যাবে" (viewable from any device) |
| "সিমলেস ইন্টিগ্রেশন" (seamless integration) | Meaningless jargon | Describe what it actually does |
| "স্কেলেবল" (scalable) | Not relevant to most buyers | Avoid unless explaining multi-location growth |
| "ইনটুইটিভ ইন্টারফেস" (intuitive interface) | Every product says this | "সহজ — ১৫ মিনিটেই শিখতে পারবেন" (easy — learn in 15 minutes) |
| "ওয়ার্ল্ড-ক্লাস" (world-class) | Sounds hollow | Skip it entirely |

## Message structure for Bangladesh (same formula, local examples)

1. **Hook** — State a problem they immediately recognise
   > "মাস শেষে বুঝতে পারছেন না লাভ হলো কি না?"
   (Can't tell at month end whether you made a profit?)

2. **Agitate** — Make the cost real
   > "প্রতিটা বিক্রয়ে কত লাভ হলো সেটা না জানলে, কোন পণ্য বেশি রাখবেন সেটা কীভাবে ঠিক করবেন?"
   (If you don't know profit on each sale, how do you decide which products to stock more of?)

3. **Solution** — Introduce Easiness
   > "Easiness প্রতিটা বিক্রয়ে স্বয়ংক্রিয়ভাবে লাভ হিসাব করে।"
   (Easiness calculates profit automatically on every sale.)

4. **Proof** — A feature, number, or example
   > "শার্ট বেচলেন ৳500, কেনা ছিল ৳320 — Easiness সাথে সাথে দেখাবে লাভ ৳180।"
   (Sold a shirt for ৳500, bought for ৳320 — Easiness instantly shows profit of ৳180.)

5. **Call to action** — Specific and easy
   > "১৪ দিন ফ্রি ব্যবহার করুন। bKash-এ কোনো পেমেন্ট লাগবে না।"
   (Use free for 14 days. No bKash payment required.)

## Emoji usage

Bangladeshi social media content (especially Facebook) commonly uses emojis.
Unlike the global brand voice (which avoids emojis unless asked), Bangladesh Facebook posts
can use emojis sparingly to draw attention to key points.

**Acceptable:**
- ✅ for bullet points about features
- 📦 for stock/inventory content
- 💰 for profit/money content
- 📱 for mobile access content
- 🔴 for problems/pain points (used once per post max)

**Avoid:**
- Overloading posts with emojis (more than 3–4 per post)
- Emojis in email or WhatsApp outreach to B2B contacts
