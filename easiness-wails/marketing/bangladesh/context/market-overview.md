# Bangladesh Market Overview

This file describes the Bangladeshi market context for selling Easiness.
Read this before creating any marketing content for this market.

---

## Market size

Bangladesh has approximately 7.8 million small and medium enterprises (SMEs).
The vast majority are in retail trade, wholesale, and import/export.

Key sectors where Easiness fits:
- **Retail grocery and daily goods** — thousands of shops in every town and city
- **Clothing and garments retail** — large segment, especially in Dhaka and Chittagong
- **Pharmacy and medical supplies** — heavily regulated, need precise stock records
- **Electronics and accessories** — high-value products where stock accuracy is critical
- **Wholesale food and FMCG** — distributors supplying hundreds of retail shops
- **Fabric and textile trading** — major in Dhaka (Islampur market), Narayanganj
- **Hardware and construction materials** — growing with infrastructure development
- **Import/export and C&F agents** — concentrated in Chittagong, Dhaka

---

## Key facts about this market

### Payment
- **bKash** — dominant mobile financial service; 60M+ registered accounts
- **Nagad** — government-backed; growing fast; strong outside Dhaka
- **Rocket (DBBL)** — established mobile banking
- **Bank transfer** — used by larger businesses; slow for small transactions
- **Cash** — still dominant for in-person transactions
- **Credit/debit card** — rare among small business owners; do NOT require it

### Internet and infrastructure
- Broadband penetration is growing but unreliable in many areas
- Mobile data (4G) is widely available but can be slow or patchy
- **Load shedding** (scheduled power cuts) still affects some areas — offline-first is a real advantage
- Most small business owners have smartphones; many also have a laptop or desktop at their shop

### Social media and digital behaviour
- **Facebook** — by far the most used platform; used daily by business owners for personal and business purposes
- **YouTube** — heavily used for video content; product demos work well here
- **WhatsApp** — widely used for business communication
- **Facebook Messenger** — used for direct business conversations
- **LinkedIn** — used mostly by corporate/professional class; less relevant for Segment 1, relevant for Segment 2 and 3
- **Instagram** — growing but less used for B2B
- **TikTok** — very popular but mostly for entertainment; less effective for B2B software

### Business culture
- Personal trust matters more than brand — a recommendation from a friend carries more weight than an ad
- Decision-making is quick for small purchases; if a shop owner sees it and understands it, they will buy the same day
- Negotiation is expected — having a yearly option with a clear discount helps
- WhatsApp and Messenger are the standard way to handle business enquiries

## Competitive landscape in Bangladesh

| What they currently use | Your opportunity |
|------------------------|-----------------|
| Handwritten ledger (খাতা) | Very easy to replace — show one feature and they see the value immediately |
| Basic Excel on shared computer | Formulas break; no profit tracking; easy to pitch against |
| Tally ERP | Expensive, complex, overkill for small businesses; price comparison wins |
| "দোকান সফটওয়্যার" (local shop software) | Often single-purpose (just POS or just invoicing); Easiness is all-in-one |
| No software at all | Large untapped segment; the first product they see and understand will win |

**Tally ERP pricing comparison (important for marketing):**
Tally Prime in Bangladesh costs approximately ৳25,000–৳35,000 for a one-time license.
Easiness Offline is ৳600/year. This is a powerful comparison.

## YouTube strategy for Bangladesh

YouTube works very well for software demos in Bangladesh.

**What to make:**
1. A 3-minute demo video: "Easiness দিয়ে দোকানের স্টক ম্যানেজ করুন" (Manage shop stock with Easiness)
2. A 5-minute tutorial: "কিভাবে Easiness সেটআপ করবেন" (How to set up Easiness)
3. Short clips (60 seconds): one feature per video (stock update, invoice creation, profit dashboard)

**Language:** Bengali with on-screen text. Speak in clear, conversational Bangla.

**Channel name suggestion:** "Easiness BD" or "Easiness - ব্যবসার সহায়ক"

## Trust-building tactics specific to Bangladesh

1. **Show the product in Bengali** — if the UI can be localised to Bengali, this dramatically increases adoption for Segment 1
2. **Video testimonials** — a 30-second video of a shop owner saying "এটা ব্যবহার করে আমার অনেক সুবিধা হয়েছে" (I've greatly benefited from using this) is worth more than any ad
3. **Demo at the customer's location** — visiting a shop owner and showing them on their own computer converts extremely well
4. **Reference local use** — "Already used by shops in Mirpur, Mohammadpur, and Dhanmondi"
5. **bKash payment** — the moment you say "bKash-এ পেমেন্ট করা যাবে", the barrier drops significantly
