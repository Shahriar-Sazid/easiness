# Segment 2 — Wholesale & Distribution Businesses (Bangladesh)

**Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH**
**Reach via: Facebook, LinkedIn, email, trade associations, referrals from Segment 1**
**Language: Bengali or English**

---

## Who this person is

A wholesale or distribution business owner in Bangladesh buys goods in bulk from manufacturers
or importers and sells to retailers, small shops, or institutions.

**Typical businesses:**
- Food and FMCG distributors (biscuits, beverages, spices, edible oil)
- Household goods wholesalers
- Textile and fabric wholesalers (Islampur, Narayanganj)
- Pharmaceutical wholesalers (non-prescription)
- Electronics parts and accessories wholesalers
- Agricultural commodity traders
- Paper and stationery distributors
- Cement, paint, and building materials suppliers

**Hubs:** Dhaka (Islampur, Bangabazar, Chawkbazar), Chittagong, Narayanganj

**Business size:** 3–20 staff; dedicated sales and delivery team
**Monthly revenue:** ৳5,00,000 – ৳5,00,00,000 (highly variable)
**Tech comfort:** Owner uses smartphone, email, and may use WhatsApp for order taking. Accounting may be done by a part-time accountant.

---

## Their daily reality

This person manages a far more complex operation than a retail shop:
- 30–100+ customers (retailers) many buying on credit
- 10–30+ suppliers with different payment terms
- A warehouse that needs accurate stock tracking
- Sales staff taking orders by phone or WhatsApp who don't always record things properly
- An accountant who reconciles everything at month end — a painful, slow process

**Their biggest fear:** A customer says "আমি দিয়েছি" (I already paid) and the business owner cannot prove otherwise. This happens regularly.

**Second biggest fear:** Placing a large purchase order from a supplier not knowing that stock from the last shipment hasn't sold yet.

---

## What they care about most

1. **Receivables management** — Who owes money, how much, and for how long
2. **Supplier account accuracy** — Exact balance with each supplier; payment history
3. **Stock across locations** — Warehouse vs. showroom vs. in-transit
4. **Profit per product line** — Which items carry the best margin
5. **Team visibility** — Sales staff, warehouse staff, and owner all need to see the same data

---

## Key differentiator for this segment

- Do just accounting (Tally, QuickBooks) — no inventory

- Are extremely expensive (Tally Prime: ৳25,000–৳35,000 one-time)
