## Who this person is

A trading or import/export company in Bangladesh sources goods from overseas (mostly China, India,
and Southeast Asia) or locally, and resells to businesses across the country.

**Typical businesses:**
- General trading companies (আমদানিকারক/রপ্তানিকারক)
- C&F (Clearing and Forwarding) agents handling imports
- Commodity importers (electronics, raw materials, chemicals)
- Textile and garment accessories importers
- Consumer goods importers (cosmetics, household products, food)
- Agricultural exporters (rice, jute, vegetables)
- Ready-made garment (RMG) accessories suppliers

**Hubs:** Dhaka (Motijheel, Dilkusha), Chittagong (Agrabad, Khatunganj)

**Business size:** 2–20 core staff plus agents and contractors
**Language:** Business English for formal dealings; Bengali for internal communication

---

## Bangladesh-specific context

Bangladesh is one of the world's top exporters of ready-made garments.
The country also imports massive volumes of raw materials, consumer goods, and industrial equipment.

This creates a large community of trading professionals who:
- Deal in large-volume transactions where one mistake is very costly
- Handle multiple currencies (USD, EUR, CNY) but need local BDT tracking
- Have complex supplier relationships across multiple countries
- Need professional documentation: purchase orders, commercial invoices, packing lists

Easiness does not replace trade finance tools (LC, BL, customs) but it fills the critical gap
of tracking what was bought, what was sold, what is in stock, and what the profit was.

---

## What they care about most

1. **Supplier account precision** — One disputed payment with a Chinese supplier can freeze a shipment. They need a clean record.
2. **Product profitability** — After freight, duties, and local costs, which items actually made money?
3. **Customer receivables** — Their customers often buy on 30–60 day credit terms. Tracking this manually is a constant headache.
4. **Data confidentiality** — Their pricing, supplier contacts, and margins are competitively sensitive.
5. **Professional documents** — They need to issue proper PDF purchase orders and invoices that meet business standards.
