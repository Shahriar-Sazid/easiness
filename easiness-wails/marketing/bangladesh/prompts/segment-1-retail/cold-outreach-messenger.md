## When to use Messenger outreach

Use Facebook Messenger to reach shop owners who:
- Are in a Facebook group for small businesses or shop owners
- Have a Facebook business page for their shop
- Have engaged with (liked or commented on) a post about small business software

Messenger is more personal than a post and more acceptable than a cold phone call.

---

## Prompt

```
You are a Bengali-language marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, Bangladesh pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a 3-message Messenger outreach sequence in Bengali for approaching a retail shop owner
who is a member of the same Facebook group but has not heard of Easiness before.

Context:
- The person owns a retail shop (type may be unspecified or a general grocery/clothing/hardware shop)
- You are reaching out after seeing their activity in a shared Facebook business group
- You are a real person, not a company bot — write accordingly

Requirements:
- Write in friendly, conversational Bengali
- Each message must be under 60 words (Messenger messages should be short)
- Message 1: Not promotional. Just a friendly question to start a conversation.
- Message 2 (if they reply positively): Introduce Easiness briefly. One key benefit relevant to a shop owner.
- Message 3 (after they show interest): Full pitch with price, trial offer, and bKash payment info.
- Sound like a real person — not a sales script
- Use "আপনি" (respectful you)
- Do NOT open with product promotion in Message 1

Output format:
মেসেজ ১ (প্রথম যোগাযোগ):
[Bengali text]

মেসেজ ২ (যদি সাড়া দেন):
[Bengali text]

মেসেজ ৩ (যদি আগ্রহ দেখান):
[Bengali text]
```

## Messenger outreach — practical tips

### Before messaging

- **Look at their profile first.** If their Facebook profile or page shows what kind of shop they run, mention it specifically: "আপনার ফার্মেসি দেখলাম" (I saw your pharmacy). Personalisation doubles reply rates.
- **Only message after a genuine interaction.** If they commented on a post you also commented on, your message feels less like spam.
- **Do NOT use copy-paste for 50 people at once.** Facebook will flag your account for spam. Send 5–10 per day maximum.

### The conversation flow

1. Send Message 1. Wait 24 hours for a reply.
2. If they reply positively → send Message 2 within 1 hour.
3. If no reply → do not follow up on Messenger. Move to WhatsApp if you have their number, or email.
4. If they reply with interest → send Message 3 and offer to do a screen share demo.

### Converting a Messenger conversation to a demo

Once they say "দেখতে চাই" (want to see it) or "কীভাবে কাজ করে?" (how does it work?):

> "আপনি কি এখন ৫ মিনিট সময় আছে? WhatsApp video call-এ screen share করে দেখাতে পারি।
> নাহলে একটা ছোট video পাঠাই?"

(Do you have 5 minutes now? I can show you via WhatsApp video call screen share.
Or I can send a short video?)

A live demo converts significantly better than any written message.
Aim for a 10-minute screen share, not a long sales call.
