# Prompt: Facebook Posts in English — Segment 1 (Retail, Bangladesh)

**Use this if you prefer to write in English or want to reach a slightly more formal audience.**
For maximum reach with Bangladeshi shop owners, prefer the Bengali version.

**Before using this prompt:** Upload these files to Claude or NotebookLM:
1. `marketing/context/product-overview.md`
2. `marketing/context/features-and-benefits.md`
3. `marketing/bangladesh/context/pricing-bangladesh.md`
4. `marketing/bangladesh/context/brand-voice-bd.md`
5. `marketing/bangladesh/customers/segment-1-retail-bd.md`

---

## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, pricing for Bangladesh, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a series of 5 Facebook posts in English targeting small retail shop owners in Bangladesh.

Requirements:
- Write in simple, clear English appropriate for a Bangladeshi audience
  (avoid idioms, slang, or culturally Western references)
- Keep each post between 80–130 words
- Use the Hook → Agitate → Solution → Proof → CTA structure
- Each post focuses on a different benefit:
  Post 1: Stock control — never run out of stock unexpectedly
  Post 2: Profit tracking — know profit on every sale automatically
  Post 3: Supplier balances — know exactly what you owe each supplier
  Post 4: Credit customers — know who owes you and how much
  Post 5: Works without internet — load-shedding proof
- End each post with a specific CTA mentioning: 14-day free trial, bKash payment accepted, or WhatsApp contact
- Mention the price: only ৳60/month or ৳600/year (Offline plan)
- You may use 2–3 emojis per post where they add meaning
- Do NOT use: "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "streamline", "cutting-edge"

Output format:
Post 1 — Stock Control:
[post text]

Post 2 — Profit Per Sale:
[post text]

Post 3 — Supplier Balances:
[post text]

Post 4 — Credit Customers:
[post text]

Post 5 — Works Offline:
[post text]
```

## When to use English vs Bengali on Facebook

| Situation | Language |
|-----------|----------|
| Targeting local market-area shop owners (Mirpur, New Market, Islampur) | Bengali |
| Targeting educated business owners who are comfortable in English | English |
| Running paid Facebook ads with broader targeting | Bengali gets more clicks from local audience |
| Posting in general entrepreneur groups | English or bilingual |

**Tip:** You can post both versions of the same post — one in Bengali, one in English — on different days.
Track which gets more engagement. Over time you will know which language your specific audience prefers.
