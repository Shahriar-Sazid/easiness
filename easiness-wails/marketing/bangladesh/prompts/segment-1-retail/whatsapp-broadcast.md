## Prompt

```
You are a Bengali-language marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, Bangladesh pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a WhatsApp broadcast message sequence (4 messages) in Bengali for reaching retail shop owners in Bangladesh.

These messages will be sent to shop owners who are in the same business network or local area.
They have not specifically asked to receive these messages, so the first message must be polite and not pushy.

Requirements for all messages:
- Write in conversational Bengali (চলিত ভাষা) — as if a real person is typing on their phone
- Address recipient as "ভাই" or "আপা" depending on context, or use their name: "[নাম] ভাই/আপা"
- Each message must be under 80 words — WhatsApp is a mobile channel
- Sound like a real person, not a company broadcast
- Do NOT use: "মাননীয়", "জনাব", "বিজ্ঞ", or any formal/official language
- Include a clear, single action at the end of each message
- Last message should offer a gentle close: "না হলে কিছু মনে করবেন না" (no problem if not interested)

Specific message purposes:
- Message 1 (Day 1): Friendly introduction. Who you are, what the app does in one sentence. One question.
- Message 2 (Day 6, if no reply): A real problem. Ask if it applies to them.
- Message 3 (Day 11, if no reply): A specific feature + easy next step (demo or trial).
- Message 4 (Day 16, if no reply): Brief, respectful close. Optional: a limited-time offer.

Price to mention: ৳60/মাস (Offline), bKash-এ পেমেন্ট।

Output format:
মেসেজ ১:
[Bengali text]

মেসেজ ২:
[Bengali text]

মেসেজ ৩:
[Bengali text]

মেসেজ ৪:
[Bengali text]
```

## How to use WhatsApp for outreach in Bangladesh

### Building your contact list (ethically)

- **Your existing network:** Friends, family, and acquaintances who own shops
- **Customers who bought from you:** Always add them with permission
- **Local business groups:** At community business events, collect numbers with a simple sheet: "Want software tips for your shop? Add your number."
- **Facebook leads:** When someone engages with your Facebook post, message them on Messenger first. If they respond, ask if you can continue on WhatsApp.

### WhatsApp Business setup (required)

1. Download **WhatsApp Business** (free) from Play Store or App Store
2. Set up your profile:
   - Business name: "Easiness"
   - Category: "Software"
   - Description: "দোকানের স্টক, ক্রয়, বিক্রয় ও লাভের সফটওয়্যার — ৳60/মাস"
   - Add your website or a link to a demo video
3. Create a **Broadcast List** with up to 256 contacts
   - Note: recipients only receive the message if they have saved your number
   - Ask new contacts to save your number when you first interact

### Sending broadcast messages

- Recipients receive the broadcast as a direct message (they don't see others)
- Replies come back to you privately — handle each one individually
- Do not send more than 1 message per week to the same list
- Space messages 5–7 days apart (as the prompt suggests)

### What to do when someone replies

Reply within 30 minutes if possible — WhatsApp is a real-time channel and quick responses convert much better.

If they say "দাম কত?" (how much?) — reply with the full pricing table:
> "Offline Plan: ৳60/মাস বা ৳600/বছর
> Sync Plan (মোবাইল থেকেও দেখা যাবে): ৳180/মাস বা ৳1,800/বছর
> ১৪ দিন ফ্রি ট্রায়াল আছে। bKash-এ পেমেন্ট করা যাবে।"

If they say "দেখাও" or "demo দাও" — offer to do a screen share via WhatsApp video call or send a YouTube demo link.
