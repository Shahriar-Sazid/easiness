## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, Bangladesh pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a cold email sequence (3 emails) targeting wholesale and distribution business owners in Bangladesh.

Language: Write in English. Wholesale business owners in Bangladesh generally use English for
formal business communication, especially email.

Context:
- Email 1: First contact — they have never heard of Easiness
- Email 2: Follow-up if no reply (send 5 days later)
- Email 3: Final follow-up if still no reply (send 5 days after Email 2)

Key facts to reference in the emails:
- Easiness Sync Plan costs ৳1,800/year — compare to Tally Prime which costs ৳25,000–৳35,000
- Specifically relevant pain: tracking receivables across many customers, supplier account accuracy
- Easiness includes inventory management — most accounting software in Bangladesh does not
- Works offline; optional cloud sync for team access
- bKash and bank transfer payment accepted

Requirements:
- Keep each email under 180 words
- Tone: professional, peer-to-peer, direct — like a message from a fellow business person
- Do NOT use: "I hope this email finds you well", "robust", "seamless", "leverage", "synergy"
- Each email leads with a different, specific business problem
- CTA is always low-friction: "Would a 15-minute call be worth your time?" or "Can I send you a demo?"
- Email 3 is short and gives them an easy out: "If this isn't relevant right now, just say the word."

Output format:
EMAIL 1
Subject: [subject line]
Body:
[email text]

---

EMAIL 2
Subject: [subject line]
Body:
[email text]

---

EMAIL 3
Subject: [subject line]
Body:
[email text]
```

## Finding wholesale business emails in Bangladesh

**Sources:**
- **DCCI (Dhaka Chamber of Commerce):** dcci.org.bd — member directory
- **MCCI (Metropolitan Chamber):** mcci-bd.org — member directory
- **BFCCI and sector chambers:** Various sector-specific chambers list members
- **Google Maps:** Search "wholesale [product] Dhaka" → visit website → find contact email
- **LinkedIn:** Many wholesale business owners have LinkedIn profiles with email or Messenger contact

**Email verification:**
Always verify emails before sending — invalid emails hurt your sender reputation.
Free tools: Hunter.io (50 free searches/month), NeverBounce (free tier available).

**Follow-up via WhatsApp/Messenger:**
If email gets no response after the 3-email sequence, find them on Facebook or LinkedIn and send a brief Messenger message referencing the email:

> "আমি [নাম] — আপনাকে কিছুদিন আগে email করেছিলাম Easiness সফটওয়্যার নিয়ে।
> ব্যস্ততার কারণে হয়তো দেখা হয়নি। ৫ মিনিট সময় হলে একটু দেখাতে চাইতাম।"
