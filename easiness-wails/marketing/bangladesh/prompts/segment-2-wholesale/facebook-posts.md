## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, Bangladesh pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a series of 5 Facebook posts targeting wholesale and distribution business owners in Bangladesh.
These are businesses that buy from manufacturers/importers and sell to retailers.

Language: Write in Bengali OR a natural Bengali-English mix (most professional wholesalers
in Bangladesh are comfortable with both). If unsure, prefer Bengali.

Requirements:
- Each post 100–160 words
- Use the brand voice: professional but human; not corporate
- Lead each post with a strong single-sentence hook
- Use short paragraphs — 1 to 3 lines each
- Each post focuses on a different topic:
  Post 1: The receivables problem — knowing who owes you money across many accounts
  Post 2: Supplier account management — proving payment history when a dispute happens
  Post 3: Stock visibility — warehouse and multiple locations
  Post 4: Tally comparison — same outcome, fraction of the price
  Post 5: Team access — salesperson + warehouse + owner on the same data
- End with a question to the audience OR a specific CTA
- Mention the price: ৳1,800/বছর (Sync Plan) for this audience
- Do NOT use: "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge"

Output format:

[post text]

[post text]
```

## Paid Facebook advertising for this segment

If organic posts do not reach enough of your target audience (wholesale/distribution owners
are a smaller group than retail shop owners), consider running a small paid Facebook ad.

**Ad setup for wholesale/distribution:**
- **Objective:** Lead generation or traffic to a demo booking page
- **Audience:** Bangladesh → location (Dhaka, Chittagong, Narayanganj) → age 28–55 → interests: "wholesale", "business software", "distribution", "supply chain"
- **Budget:** Start with ৳500–1,000 per campaign
- **Ad format:** Single image with the best-performing organic post as the ad copy
- **Call to action:** "Send Message" (directs to Messenger) or "Learn More" (directs to website/WhatsApp)

**What to measure:**
- Cost per message/lead — if under ৳50 per response, the ad is working
- If cost is too high, test a different post copy or a different audience interest
