## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, Bangladesh pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a cold email sequence (3 emails) targeting trading company owners and import/export
business managers in Bangladesh.

Language: English. Trading companies in Bangladesh use English as the standard language for
formal business correspondence.

Context:
- Email 1: First contact — they have never heard of Easiness
- Email 2: Follow-up if no reply (send 5 days later)
- Email 3: Final follow-up if still no reply (send 5 days after Email 2)

Key facts to reference:
- Easiness Team Plan: ৳3,600/year — compare to Tally ৳25,000–৳35,000
- Key differentiator: self-hosted option — their sensitive trade data stays on their own server
- Specifically relevant pain: knowing profit margin after all costs; supplier payment records; customer receivables
- Works offline — critical for areas with unreliable internet
- bKash and bank transfer accepted for payment

Requirements:
- Keep each email under 200 words
- Tone: professional peer-to-peer — like a message from someone who understands their industry
- Do NOT use: "I hope this email finds you well", "robust", "seamless", "leverage", "synergy", "solutions"
- Each email leads with a specific, costly business problem that trading companies face
- Mention Bangladesh context naturally: overseas suppliers, Chittagong port, credit terms
- CTA: always low-friction — "15-minute call?" or "Can I send you a demo video?"
- Email 3 is short — offer to close gracefully
```

## Finding trading company contacts in Bangladesh

**Sources:**
- **Chittagong Chamber of Commerce:** ccici.net — member directory
- **DCCI (Dhaka Chamber):** dcci.org.bd
- **BGMEA (garments):** bgmea.com.bd — member directory for RMG-related businesses
- **BKMEA (knitwear):** bkmea.com
- **Bangladesh Customs:** Import/export registration is public record in some cases
- **Alibaba supplier profiles:** Bangladeshi exporters often list their contact email on Alibaba
- **LinkedIn:** Search "import Bangladesh", "export Bangladesh", "trading Chittagong"
- **Google Maps:** "trading company Chittagong/Dhaka" → website contact pages

**Personalisation that helps for this segment:**
Before emailing, spend 2 minutes on their website or LinkedIn. If you can mention:
- Their specific product category: "I see you import electronics accessories..."
- Their location: "...based in Chittagong..."
- A real industry challenge: "...with Chinese suppliers, payment disputes can tie up a shipment for weeks..."

This level of personalisation dramatically increases reply rates in B2B email.

**Decision timeline:**
Expect a 4–8 week sales cycle from first contact to a decision.
Do not chase aggressively — follow up once per week maximum.
If they say "not now", ask: "When would be a better time? I'll reach out then." and respect the answer.
