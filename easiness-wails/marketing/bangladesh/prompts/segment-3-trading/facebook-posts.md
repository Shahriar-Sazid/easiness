## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.
I have provided context files about the product, Bangladesh pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a series of 5 Facebook posts targeting trading company owners and importers/exporters in Bangladesh.
Focus on businesses concentrated in Dhaka (Motijheel) and Chittagong (Agrabad, Khatunganj).

Language: English, or a professional English-Bengali mix. This audience uses English regularly
in business communication.

Requirements:
- Each post 100–160 words
- Lead each post with a strong one-sentence hook
- Use short paragraphs — 1 to 3 lines
- Professional but human tone — not corporate
- Each post focuses on a different topic:
  Post 1: Knowing actual margin after all costs — landed cost tracking
  Post 2: Supplier dispute protection — having a clean payment record
  Post 3: Customer receivables on 30–60 day credit terms
  Post 4: Data confidentiality — self-hosted, not on someone else's server
  Post 5: Professional PDF documents — purchase orders and invoices that look right
- End with a question or a CTA (offer a 15-minute demo call or 14-day trial)
- Reference Bangladesh context: mention Chittagong port, Chinese suppliers, or BDT where natural
- Price to mention: ৳3,600/বছর (Team Plan) — compare to Tally at ৳25,000–৳35,000
- Do NOT use: "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "synergy", "end-to-end", "cutting-edge"

Output format:
Post 1 — Margin Tracking:
[post text]

Post 2 — Supplier Dispute Protection:
[post text]

Post 3 — Customer Receivables:
[post text]

Post 4 — Data Privacy:
[post text]

Post 5 — Professional Documents:
[post text]
```

## Where to distribute this content

**Facebook pages and groups for this segment:**
- Bangladesh Importers and Exporters groups
- Chittagong business community groups
- "আমদানি রপ্তানি ব্যবসা" (Import export business) groups
- Entrepreneur and business owner groups in Dhaka's Motijheel area

**LinkedIn:**
This segment is more active on LinkedIn than the retail segment.
Repurpose each Facebook post as a LinkedIn post (shorter, more formal).
See `segment-3-trading/cold-email.md` for the full LinkedIn strategy.

**YouTube:**
A 3-minute demo video specifically for trading companies — showing:
1. Creating a purchase order for an overseas supplier
2. Recording the stock arrival
3. Creating a sales invoice
4. Viewing the profit margin on that product

This type of video ranks in YouTube search and builds credibility before any outreach.
