## What is Easiness?

Easiness is a **business management desktop application** for small and medium businesses that buy from suppliers and sell to customers.

It runs on your computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux) and works even when you have no internet connection. When you are online, it can sync data to a web server so you can access it from a browser too.

It is designed to replace paper notebooks, spreadsheets, and disconnected apps that small business owners currently use to manage their operations.

## The problem it solves

Most small business owners face the same frustrations:

- "I don't know exactly how much stock I have"
- "I don't know which products are making me the most profit"
- "I don't know how much money customers owe me"
- "I lose supplier invoices and forget what I paid"
- "My Excel sheet is a mess and I'm scared of deleting something"
- "I don't know if my business is actually profitable"

Easiness solves all of these in one place, on a single computer, without needing an internet connection or a monthly subscription.

---

## Who it is for

Easiness is built for:
- Small retail shop owners (grocery, clothing, electronics, pharmacy, hardware, etc.)
- Wholesale and distribution businesses
- Small trading and import/export companies

It is **not** built for: large enterprises, multi-branch franchises, businesses that need payroll, or businesses that need tax filing integration.

---

## How it works (in plain language)

1. **You set up your products and stock locations.** Tell Easiness what you sell and where you store it.
2. **When you buy from a supplier**, you create a Purchase Order. Easiness automatically adds the stock to your inventory and updates what you owe the supplier.
3. **When you sell to a customer**, you create a Sales Invoice. Easiness automatically removes the stock and calculates your profit on that sale.
4. **All your money movements** (payments in, payments out, bank transfers) are recorded as transactions tied to your bank accounts.
5. **The dashboard** shows you your top-selling products, most profitable items, who owes you money, and what you owe others — at a glance.

## Platform

- **Desktop app:** Windows 10/11, macOS (Intel + Apple Silicon), Linux
- **Web app (optional):** Access from any browser when synced to a server
- **Database:** Stored locally on your computer (SQLite). No data sent to any third party.

---

## Pricing model

Easiness is sold as an **annual license**. The customer pays once per year and gets all updates during that year. There is no monthly subscription. When the license expires, the software stops working until a new license is purchased.

License tiers:
- **Standard** — single device, all core features
- **Professional** — desktop + web sync across multiple devices
- **Enterprise** — web access, team use, priority support
