# Easiness — Target Customer Segments

## Summary

Easiness is built for businesses that **buy from suppliers and sell to customers** and need
to track what they have, what they owe, and what they earned. Three segments are the
primary targets:

| Segment | Description | Size | Best Channel | Priority |
|---------|-------------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1. Retail Shops | Small shop owners selling directly to consumers | 1–10 staff | Facebook, WhatsApp, Word of mouth | HIGH |
| 2. Wholesale / Distribution | Businesses selling to other businesses in bulk | 2–30 staff | LinkedIn, Email, Trade associations | MEDIUM |
| 3. Trading / Import-Export | Companies importing or sourcing products to resell | 2–20 staff | LinkedIn, Email, Chamber of Commerce | MEDIUM |

---

## Why these three segments?

All three have exactly the same core workflow:
1. Buy from suppliers (Purchase Orders)
2. Store goods in one or more locations (Inventory)
3. Sell to customers (Invoices)
4. Track who owes money and who they owe (Accounts receivable/payable)

Easiness is purpose-built for this workflow.

---

## Who is NOT a target customer

- Businesses that only provide services (no physical products)
- Large enterprises with 100+ staff (need ERP)
- Restaurants (need a POS system)
- Businesses that need payroll, tax filing, or HR features
- Pure e-commerce businesses (need warehouse management + shipping integration)

---

## Starting recommendation

If you are just launching, focus on **Segment 1 (Retail Shops)** first because:
- It is the largest segment
- Shop owners have a clear, immediate pain (stock management)
- They are reachable cheaply via Facebook and local groups
- They make quick decisions once they see the product works

Once you have 20–30 retail customers and testimonials, use those as proof to
approach Segment 2 and Segment 3, who will pay more but take longer to decide.
