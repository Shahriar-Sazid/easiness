# Segment 1 — Retail Shop Owners

**Priority: HIGH**
**Reach via: Facebook, WhatsApp, local business groups, word of mouth**

---

## Who this person is

A retail shop owner runs a physical store selling products directly to consumers.
They are likely the owner and the main worker at the same time.

**Typical businesses in this segment:**
- Grocery / convenience stores
- Clothing and fashion shops
- Electronics and accessories shops
- Pharmacies and health stores
- Hardware and building materials shops
- Baby and children's goods shops
- Stationery and school supplies shops
- Cosmetics and beauty product shops

**Business size:** 1–10 staff, usually family-run
**Annual revenue:** $20,000 – $300,000 (varies widely by market)
**Tech comfort:** Basic smartphone user. Uses WhatsApp and Facebook daily.
**Computer use:** Has a laptop or desktop but may not use it heavily for business.

---

## Their daily reality

This person wakes up, opens the shop, and immediately faces questions they cannot answer confidently:

- Did we run out of [product] yesterday? I need to order more.
- A supplier called — how much do I owe them?
- A customer says they paid last week. Did they? How much?
- It's end of month. Did I make money or lose money? I have no idea.

They are busy. They are the manager, the salesperson, the stock controller, and the cashier — often all at once. They do not have time to learn complicated software. They want something that works and saves them from stress, not something that adds to it.

---

## How they currently manage (the competition you are replacing)

| What they use | Why it fails them |
|---------------|------------------|
| Handwritten notebook | Lost, illegible, no search, no totals, can't be backed up |
| Excel spreadsheet | Formulas break, one wrong cell corrupts everything, easy to delete, no profit calculation |
| Memory alone | "I think we have 3 left" — leads to stockouts and over-buying |
| Multiple WhatsApp groups | Orders scattered in chats, no record, no structure |
| Basic invoice app | Does invoices only — no stock, no accounts, no profit tracking |

---

## What they care about most (in order)

1. **Stock control** — They lose money when they run out of fast-selling items, and waste money when slow items pile up. This is their #1 pain.
2. **Knowing who owes them money** — Credit sales to regular customers are common. They often forget who hasn't paid.
3. **Knowing what they owe suppliers** — Suppliers sometimes add items they didn't notice, or they forget a partial payment.
4. **Profit per product** — Which products are actually making them money? They often don't know.
5. **Speed** — They cannot stop a sale to fiddle with software. Creating an invoice must take under 60 seconds.

---

## What they are afraid of (purchase objections)

- "I'm not good with computers" → Show them it's as simple as filling in a form
- "I don't have time to learn new software" → Tell them setup takes 30 minutes; show the UI is in plain language
- "It's too expensive" → "$29 for a whole year is less than the cost of one stockout"
- "I'll just keep using Excel" → Ask them: "How many hours per week do you spend updating it manually?"
- "What if I lose my data?" → Explain: data is on their computer; optional cloud backup available
- "Will it work when my internet is down?" → Yes. It is fully offline. Works always.

---

## What success looks like for them

After 30 days of using Easiness, they should be able to say:
- "I know exactly how much stock I have without counting"
- "I can see which product made me the most profit last month"
- "I know exactly what I owe each supplier"
- "I can see who hasn't paid me and how much they owe"
- "I know my cash balance across all accounts"

## Best messages for this segment

**Primary message:** Stock control
> You don't know exactly what you have. You run out of things you should have, and you have too much of things that don't sell. Easiness fixes that.

**Secondary message:** Know your profit
> Working hard every day but not sure if you're making money? Easiness shows you the profit on every sale automatically.

**Tertiary message:** Professional look
> Send your customers a proper PDF invoice by WhatsApp instead of a handwritten receipt. It takes 60 seconds.
