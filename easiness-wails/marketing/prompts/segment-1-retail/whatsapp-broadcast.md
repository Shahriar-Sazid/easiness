## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.

I have provided you with context files about the product, its features and benefits, pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a WhatsApp broadcast message sequence (4 messages) for reaching retail shop owners.

These messages will be sent to a list of shop owners who have given consent to receive messages (e.g. from a business network group or a trade association list).

Requirements for all four messages:
- Each message must be under 100 words — WhatsApp is not email
- Use first names where possible: start with "Hi [Name]"
- Sound like a real person sending from their phone — not a company broadcast
- Do NOT include emojis unless they add meaning
- Each message should have a clear, single call to action — reply, click a link, or answer a question
- Keep it conversational — short sentences, plain words
- Do NOT use: "Dear Business Owner", "Please be informed", "I would like to inform you", "kindly"

Specific message purposes:
- Message 1: Introduction — who you are, what the app does, one question to get a reply
- Message 2 (send 5 days later if no reply): A real problem they face — ask if it applies to them
- Message 3 (send 5 days after Message 2): A specific feature + demo offer
- Message 4 (send 5 days after Message 3): Final message — time-limited offer or simple close

Output format:
MESSAGE 1
[text]

MESSAGE 2
[text]

MESSAGE 3
[text]

MESSAGE 4
[text]
```

## How WhatsApp outreach works

**Building your list:**
- Ask every new customer for their WhatsApp number when they sign up
- Get permission from local business groups to message members
- At trade events, collect numbers directly with consent

**WhatsApp Business vs personal:**
- Use WhatsApp Business (free app) for business outreach — it lets you create a broadcast list, set a business profile, and use quick replies
- Do NOT use personal WhatsApp for broadcast messages to many people — your account may be flagged

**Broadcast lists vs groups:**
- Broadcast lists: each person receives the message as a direct message (they don't see others on the list)
- Groups: everyone sees each other — use groups for community building, not cold outreach
- Use broadcast lists for this outreach

**Compliance:**
- Only message people who have saved your number or who have given consent
- Always offer a way to opt out: "Reply STOP if you don't want to hear from me"
- WhatsApp can restrict accounts that receive too many blocks

**Timing:**
- Tuesday to Thursday between 10am and 12pm or 4pm and 6pm work best
- Avoid early morning and late night

**Responding to replies:**
- Reply quickly — WhatsApp is a real-time channel
- When someone shows interest, move to a call or a screen share demo immediately
- Don't try to close a sale entirely over text — use text to get to a call
