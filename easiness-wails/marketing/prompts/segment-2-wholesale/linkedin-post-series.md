# Prompt: LinkedIn Post Series — Segment 2 (Wholesale & Distribution)

**Before using this prompt:** Upload the four context files and `segment-2-wholesalers.md` to Claude or NotebookLM.

---

## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.

I have provided you with context files about the product, its features and benefits, pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a series of 5 LinkedIn posts targeting wholesale and distribution business owners and managers.

Requirements:
- Each post should be between 100 and 200 words
- LinkedIn posts work best when they open with a short, punchy single-sentence hook (1 line) that makes people stop scrolling
- Use line breaks frequently — short paragraphs of 1–3 lines each. Never write a wall of text on LinkedIn.
- Use the brand voice guidelines: professional but human, not corporate
- Each post should focus on a different topic:
  Post 1: The receivables problem (knowing who owes you and how much)
  Post 2: Stock visibility across warehouse and sales locations
  Post 3: Supplier account management and payment tracking
  Post 4: Profit margins per product — knowing what's actually making money
  Post 5: Team access — salesperson, warehouse manager, and owner on the same live data
- End each post with either a question to the audience (encourages comments) or a CTA to try Easiness
- Do NOT use: "synergy", "leverage", "robust solution", "end-to-end", "seamless", "streamline your operations", "cutting-edge"
- Do NOT open with "I" as the first word — LinkedIn algorithm de-boosts posts starting with "I"

Output format:
POST 1 — Receivables
[post text]

POST 2 — Stock Visibility
[post text]

POST 3 — Supplier Accounts
[post text]

POST 4 — Profit Margins
[post text]

POST 5 — Team Access
[post text]
```

## LinkedIn best practices for this audience

**Profile before posting:**
Before posting, make sure your LinkedIn profile is complete:
- Professional photo
- Headline that says what Easiness does: "Founder @ Easiness | Business management software for distributors and wholesalers"
- About section that uses the one-line pitch from `product-overview.md`

**Posting schedule:**
- 2–3 posts per week is optimal for LinkedIn growth
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 8–10am or 12–2pm

**Engagement strategy:**
- Reply to every comment on your posts within 2 hours
- Comments boost the algorithm significantly — ask a question at the end of every post
- Comment thoughtfully on posts from people in your target segment — this builds visibility

**LinkedIn DM outreach:**
After someone engages with your post (likes, comments), send them a connection request with a personalized note, then follow up with a brief DM after they accept.

**Hashtags to use:**
`#distribution` `#wholesale` `#supplychainmanagement` `#smallbusiness` `#inventorymanagement` `#businesssoftware`

Use 3–5 hashtags per post, placed at the very end.
