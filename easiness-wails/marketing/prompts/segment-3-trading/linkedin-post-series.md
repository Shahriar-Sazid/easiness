## Prompt

```
You are a marketing copywriter for a software product called Easiness.

I have provided you with context files about the product, its features and benefits, pricing, brand voice, and the target customer.

Write a series of 5 LinkedIn posts targeting trading company owners, importers, and export agents.

Requirements:
- Each post should be between 100 and 200 words
- Open with a short, punchy single-sentence hook (1 line) that makes people stop scrolling
- Use line breaks — short paragraphs of 1–3 lines. No walls of text.
- Use the brand voice guidelines: professional but human, not corporate
- Speak directly to the realities of trading: shipments, landed costs, overseas suppliers, credit terms
- Each post should focus on a different topic:
  Post 1: The hidden profit problem — you close a deal but don't know the real margin until much later
  Post 2: Supplier account accuracy — disputed invoices and how to protect yourself
  Post 3: Customer receivables on credit terms — who owes you money after 30/60/90 days
  Post 4: Data privacy — your trade data should not be on someone else's cloud server
  Post 5: Professional documents — the gap between a handwritten note and a proper PDF purchase order
- End each post with a question to the audience or a CTA
- Do NOT use: "synergy", "leverage", "robust", "seamless", "end-to-end", "cutting-edge"
- Do NOT start with "I" as the first word

Output format:
POST 1 — Hidden Profit Problem
[post text]

POST 2 — Supplier Account Accuracy
[post text]

POST 3 — Customer Receivables
[post text]

POST 4 — Data Privacy
[post text]

POST 5 — Professional Documents
[post text]
```

## LinkedIn best practices for this audience

**Who to target on LinkedIn:**
Search terms that find your audience:
- Job titles: "trader", "import manager", "export director", "sourcing manager", "trading company director"
- Industry filters: Import and Export, International Trade, Wholesale
- Company size filter: 2–50 employees

**Content strategy for trading audience:**
Trading company owners respond well to:
- Real-world cost examples ("One disputed invoice on a $50,000 shipment...")
- Privacy and data security messaging (they handle sensitive supplier and pricing data)
- Speed and simplicity messaging (they are often managing multiple deals simultaneously)

**Hashtags to use:**
`#importexport` `#trading` `#tradefinance` `#supplychain` `#commoditytrading` `#businessmanagement`

**Building an audience:**
Connect with people who engage with your posts. When someone likes or comments, look at their profile — if they are in your target segment, send a personalized connection request:

> "Hi [Name], saw you engaged with my post about supplier reconciliation — I work in a similar space. Would be great to connect."

After connecting, wait 3–5 days before sending any message about Easiness.
